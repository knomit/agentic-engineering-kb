# Expect a 5-10% factual-inconsistency floor; below 2% is hard even on simple summarization

Practitioners report a baseline hallucination rate of 5-10% factual inconsistency, and that pushing below 2% is difficult even for a task as constrained as summarizing a provided document — where every fact needed is in the input.

Why this changes what you build: it makes "reduce hallucinations with a better prompt" a bounded strategy with a floor well above zero, so any product whose correctness requirement is stricter than ~2% cannot be met by generation alone. You need a downstream factual-consistency check as a separate component, combined with upstream chain-of-thought. Design the surrounding workflow — citation, verification, human confirmation on the consequential step — to absorb a residual error rate that will not go away.

Do not read this as a fixed constant for all models and tasks; it is a practitioner-reported order of magnitude for grounded summarization. Read it as: budget for a floor, and measure your own rather than assuming yours is zero.
