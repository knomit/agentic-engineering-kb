# The live OWASP agentic incident tracker's headline is three populations stacked — 513 records, of which 54 are curated incidents and 455 a retroactive NVD High/Critical keyword sweep

The live OWASP Agentic AI Security Incidents & Exploits Tracker, as the page displayed itself on 2026-10-19 (read in a browser; the page serves no content to a plain fetch), states "513 Records Tracked" and then splits that total into three populations with different provenance and different selection rules:

- **Curated incidents: 54** — OWASP repository entries, counted by incident month. These "require manual administrator approval" and "may describe misuse or exploits without a CVE".
- **Verified CVE discovery: 4** — authoritative sources, counted by CVE publication month. Candidates are found by a scheduled Perplexity search and published AUTOMATICALLY once they have a unique CVE, a non-rejected NVD record, a CVSS score and agentic relevance.
- **Retroactive NVD: 455** — Jan-Sep 2026, selected for High/Critical severity.

The tracker's own charts and CSV downloads follow the selected population, and its approved-records-by-month chart EXCLUDES the retroactive NVD entries entirely, giving a recorded period of Feb '25 - Dec '25 for the curated series. Classification status is stated and weak: of the 54, there are 54 source-provided mappings, 0 automated and 0 unmapped, and "Independent classification review is not recorded". ASI02 Tool Misuse is the most frequent mapping at 30 records, 56% of selected records, and the page itself says that "is not a measure of real-world prevalence", that counts are not an incident rate, and that vulnerabilities do not establish exploitation. One record can map to several ASI categories, so the bars sum to more than the record count.

CITE THE POPULATION, NEVER THE HEADLINE. "513 agentic AI security incidents" is wrong three ways at once: most of the 513 are CVEs matched by keyword rather than incidents, the High/Critical filter on that bulk IS the selection bias, and no mapping in the dataset has been independently reviewed. A defensible count of documented agentic incidents from this source is the curated population, and it must carry a date, because the dataset is live with "no hosted archive or DOI" — reproducing an analysis means preparing a snapshot with its SHA-256 fingerprint and keeping the JSON yourself.

What this does NOT mean: the tracker is not unusable, and it is the most current public catalogue of its kind — each curated record links the primary source that reported it, which is where a claim about any individual incident has to come from. The discipline it demands is naming which of its three populations a number came from, and reading an ASI category share as a reporting artifact rather than a threat ranking.
