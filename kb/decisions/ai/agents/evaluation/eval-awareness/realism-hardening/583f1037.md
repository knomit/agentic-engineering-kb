# Hardening an audit suite against eval-awareness means stripping environment-driven cues only — the task-driven cues are the stimulus, and Petri 2.0's two interventions bought a 47.3% median relative drop in verbalized awareness while reducing unprompted initiative

Petri 2.0 (Anthropic Alignment Science, August 2026) splits the cues that make a target model announce it is being tested into two kinds and treats them in OPPOSITE directions: "we distinguish between *task-driven* and *environment-driven* eval-awareness."

Task-driven cues are the ones the scenario needs in order to elicit the behaviour under test — the post names "giving the model unusual authority, access to sensitive information, or contradictory goals" — and they are KEPT, because removing them removes the stimulus. Environment-driven cues are the implausibilities of the staging: implausible user behaviour, inconsistent tool responses, obvious honeypots. Only those are reduced. Two mechanisms do the reducing: manual seed revision (concrete background, varied names and locations, removing language implying no oversight, and moving the opportunity for bad behaviour later and making it subtler), and a prompted realism classifier that checks each auditor turn before it reaches the target model and can demand a rewrite.

The two were ablated separately and together, across 10 target models ("we ran Petri across 10 target models"), two auditors ("using two different auditors (Claude Sonnet 4.5 and GPT-5.1)") and three independent judges ("each resulting transcript was then independently judged by Claude Opus 4.5, Gemini 3 Pro, and GPT-5.1"). Seeds alone: "21.7% drop with just the new seeds". Classifier alone: "37.2% drop with just the realism classifier". Both: "On Claude models, the interventions together lead to a 47.3% median relative drop in eval-awareness".

THE COST IS ON THE SAME PAGE, AND IT IS WHAT MAKES THIS A TRADEOFF RATHER THAN AN IMPROVEMENT. Subtler, later-arriving honeypots cost behavioural signal: "we do see a decrease in unprompted initiative for several models", most strongly with both interventions applied, and some models missed the intended risk scenario altogether. So realism hardening is budgeted against elicitation rather than maximised. If a suite's job is to surface a behaviour at all, an unhardened seed that surfaces it is worth more than a realistic one that does not; if the job is to estimate a rate that a reader will take as deployment-relevant, the hardening is what buys that reading.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. It does not mean eval-awareness was removed. The measured quantity is VERBALIZED awareness — the model stating in its reasoning that it is being tested. The authors expect the interventions to reduce non-verbalized forms as well but say "we do not yet have concrete evidence for how large this effect is", so the effect on a model that notices and says nothing is unmeasured here. It also does not mean the two-cue line is crisp: the post states the distinction is not always clear, so the classifier can over- or under-trigger, and a classifier tuned to strip environment cues can take a task cue with it.
